# Number Hop

**Play it here: https://innerloz.github.io/number-hop-play/**

A free little number game for young kids. You are a friendly block character
standing on a board of numbers: hop onto a number that is **smaller than you** and
it is added to you. Keep growing! Easy goes up to 1,000, Hard all the way to
1,000,000, and there are a few secret surprises on the way.

Start by choosing Charlie, George, Henry, Dylan, Anna or William from their block character
portraits, then pick a level. Your portrait and name stay with you while you play.
Use **Change player** on the title or pause menu when it's someone else's turn.

Each player starts as their age: **Charlie 8**, **George 4**, **Henry 6**,
**Dylan 5**, **Anna 2**, and **William 3**. This applies on every level and replay.
Smaller starter tiles help Anna and William get growing straight away.

Henry has a special **sixth birthday** welcome: a birthday jingle and a cake with
six candles and a big 6! Choose his portrait to celebrate, then pick a level.

**Extreme** has addition, subtraction and **×2, ×3, ×4 and ×5** tiles. Extra small
subtractions help you correct your total; division and square-root tiles are removed.
Land exactly on **100, 1,000, 10,000, 100,000 or 1,000,000** for a special
achievement and celebration (up to your level's goal). Subtraction counts too!
Each achievement celebrates once per round.

* **Keyboard:** arrow keys (or WASD) to walk, **Space** to hop, Esc to pause, M to mute.
* **Phone / tablet:** drag anywhere in the play area to walk, lift to stop, and tap
  anywhere to hop. Tap with a second finger to hop while steering. The pictured
  arrow pad and **HOP** button still work. Landscape works best. You can add it
  to your home screen.

Made with [Godot](https://godotengine.org). Fonts: Nunito and Titan One (SIL Open
Font License). Everything else (models, textures, sounds, music) was made for this
game.

This is the built game only. It is an unofficial, non-commercial fan game made for
a child at home, inspired by a well-known children's TV show about number blocks;
it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the show's makers.
